How the tax works

Only the gain inside a withdrawal is taxed. The part that is your own contributions coming back is never taxed again, so every withdrawal is split pro-rata between the two — the same way a brokerage reports it. The income you enter is what you actually receive, so the withdrawal is grossed up to cover the tax.
