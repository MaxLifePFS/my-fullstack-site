Before acting on this: a qualified defined benefit plan is not funded by a single deposit of your choosing. An enrolled actuary sets the contribution each year from the plan's benefit formula, your compensation history and the plan's own assumed rate — the figure above is what the promise is worth today at the rate you entered, which is the starting point for that work, not a substitute for it. Two statutory limits also bind: the annual benefit is capped by IRC 415(b) (indexed each year, recently around the $280,000 mark) and by your high-three-year average compensation. Deductibility has its own limits and the deduction is a deferral, not forgiveness — every dollar of income drawn later is taxed as ordinary income then.
