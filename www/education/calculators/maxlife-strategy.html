Inputs — income never leaves the asset bucket

All flows happen at the beginning of the year, then assets and properties grow for that year. Borrowing each year takes whatever headroom is left under the loan-to-value cap. Rental income pays the loan interest first; whatever is left over pours back into the asset bucket. If the rent does not cover the interest, the shortfall is added to the loan.
